For Apple Mail on macOS

Less political email.
More room for your inbox.

Mail-Filter analyzes incoming messages on your Mac and moves verified political solicitations to Junk. You choose the accounts, tune the filtering instructions, and stay in control.

Analysis stays on your Mac. Classification uses Apple's on-device model. Mail-Filter has no advertising, analytics or service that uploads email for analysis.

What you need

macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence enabled and its model available. Mail must remain running and your Mac must be awake. The Mail-Filter settings app can close.

The filter handles new arrivals. AI can make mistakes; check Junk periodically and move false positives back to Inbox.
